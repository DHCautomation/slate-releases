Phase D of 0.3.0: MQTT, Modbus TCP devices, served BACnet objects, and online updates with release notes, channels and rollback.

- MQTT: points published on change (with a deadband) and in full every few minutes, alarms and status; writes by subscribe reach only points tagged mqtt-write, at a chosen priority and rate limited; Home Assistant discovery (off by default); a site CA for TLS.
- Modbus TCP: up to 8 devices (host, port, unit) on the same point model as Modbus RTU; a device that stops answering goes offline.
- Served objects: every local point is a BACnet object a supervisory system can read; the database revision survives a restart and every object has a description.
- Updates: System > Updates checks the release server (stable or beta channel), shows the notes of every version in between, installs with progress, and rolls back to the other slot. An install window for stable updates is off by default.
- Three crashes within 10 minutes of a restart after an update and the SlateTap goes back to the previous firmware by itself.
- What is new: a card on Home after an update.
- Back on 0.2.0 there is no MQTT and no Modbus TCP: their settings wait unused and return with 0.3.0.
